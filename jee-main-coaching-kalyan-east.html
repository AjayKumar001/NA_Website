JEE Main Program

JEE Main Coaching in Kalyan East

If you are searching for JEE Main coaching classes near HM Road, Kalyan East, National Academy's Sahyadri Chambers center (near Jan Kalyan Hospital) runs dedicated JEE Main batches for Class 11, Class 12 and repeater students throughout the academic year.

Our JEE Main program follows the complete NTA syllabus for Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, with every chapter reinforced through weekly tests and full-length mock exams that mirror the actual exam pattern and difficulty curve.

Unlike large coaching chains where a single mentor may track hundreds of students, our batches are capped at 35 students so every JEE Main aspirant gets individual attention, direct access to faculty for doubt-solving, and a personalized study plan based on their weekly test performance.

Parents receive automatic weekly performance reports and meet mentors in person once a month to review accuracy, speed, concept strength and consistency — the four metrics we track for every JEE Main student.
